# Time management priors

Naroditsky cap: only spend 15+ seconds on a critical node. The control changes how many such sits you can buy and how much clock you must keep for the finish.

| Control | >=10s / game | >=15s / game | Median finish | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3+0 | 1.8 | 0.7 | ~67s | Two-second game. Bank a minute. |
| 3+1 | 4.8 | 2.5 | ~22s | Increment buys thinks, thin ending. |
| 3+2 | ~5 | ~2.7 | ~25s | Soft prior. |
| 5+0 | 6.0 | 3.3 (+0.8 of 30s) | ~61s | One 30s sit. Keep a minute. No refund. |

Phases: cheap opening, expensive moves 11-25, fast conversion. Flagging a won ending is Principle 4, not play faster everywhere.

Elite TT 5+0 still flags ~5%. Club 3+0 flag rates of 20%+ are a bottleneck, not a style.
