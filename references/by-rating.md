# Rating-band defaults

Use these only to break ties when the sample is quiet.

- Under 1600 — hanging pieces and one-move tactics first. Openings get a 10-move system, not a book.
- 1600-2000 — conversion, flags, and a tiny opening patch. Calculation on 2-3 nodes.
- 2000-2300 — flags and conversion still dominate if timeout losses exceed ~10%. Otherwise color/opening slices and candidate-move hygiene.
- 2300+ — time quotas vs the control prior, plus one opening family with n>=20.

Puzzle vs live is an archetype, not a universal. See archetypes.md.
